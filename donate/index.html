Support the dictionary

The dictionary is free to read and always will be. Voluntary donations are intended to cover maintenance and editorial work; they never affect what a definition says.

Donations are not open yet

No donation recipient or payment provider has been approved for this project, so there is nothing to pay into and no payment has been or can be taken through this site. This page exists so that the arrangements can be published and checked before any money is requested.

What contributions would support

  • hosting and domain costs
  • scientific editing and review
  • accessibility improvements
  • original illustrations
  • reviewed translations

What donations do not buy

Financial support gives no influence over scientific content, review outcomes, the choice of terms or the wording of any entry. No donor is named in an entry. Donors may decline public acknowledgement entirely, and any newsletter consent is separate from a contribution and off by default.

Transparency

Once fundraising is active, the project intends to publish a periodic summary of funds received and how they were spent, without exposing donor information. No donor counts, totals or funding targets are displayed until the underlying records are accurate and their disclosure has been authorised.

See also the donation information policy.
