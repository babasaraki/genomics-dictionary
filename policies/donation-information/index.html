Donation information

This page exists so that the donation arrangements can be checked before anyone gives anything.

Donations are not open yet

No donation recipient or payment provider has been approved for this project, so there is nothing to pay into and no payment has been or can be taken through this site. This page exists so that the arrangements can be published and checked before any money is requested.

How payments are handled

This website never collects or stores card details. When a donation facility is live, the supporter is sent to the payment provider's own hosted page, and the provider handles the transaction, confirmation and receipt. A return to this website is not treated as proof of payment, and no thank-you message is shown unless the provider has confirmed the payment.

What the project does not claim

The project makes no claim of charitable registration or tax deductibility unless and until a confirmed basis for that claim exists. No donor names, amounts, totals or progress figures are published without accurate records and explicit authorisation.
