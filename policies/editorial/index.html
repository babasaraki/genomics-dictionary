Editorial policy

This policy describes how an entry reaches the public site and what each status means. It is written to be verifiable: the site never displays a review that did not happen.

Editorial states

An entry moves through draft, submitted, under review, changes requested, approved and published. Withdrawn is a public state with a retained reason. Editorial status is recorded separately from public visibility, so a draft revision can exist while readers continue to see the last published version.

Review

A review decision attaches to the exact revision reviewed. Material changes after approval invalidate that approval and require renewed review. A launch entry must be reviewed by someone other than its primary author. Publication requires every mandatory field, references, a recorded reviewer approval and a next review date; the default re-review interval is 12 months.

Roles

ContributorCreates and revises assigned drafts and submits them for review. Cannot publish or approve their own submission.
Scientific reviewerReviews assigned revisions and records decisions. Cannot silently edit a public version.
Section editorAssigns work, requests revisions and approves subject content.
Managing editorPublishes approved revisions, schedules reviews and withdraws entries, retaining audit history.
AdministratorManages users, settings and technical access. Administrative access does not imply scientific approval.

Sources and originality

Entries are written in the project's own words. A source link is not evidence of review: references and reviewer approval are recorded separately. Term-specific authoritative sources are preferred over a general glossary homepage. Adapted text is documented, and third-party illustrations and quotations carry their own rights records.

Scientific language

Entries distinguish a variant from a pathogenic variant, genetic ancestry from social identity, association from causation, and germline from somatic findings. No genome is described as normal, ideal or representative of all populations. Acronyms with more than one meaning are disambiguated explicitly.

AI assistance

AI tools may assist with drafting and consistency checking. AI cannot confer reviewed status, approve a clinical statement or publish an entry. The present corpus was drafted with AI assistance — the contributor field on every entry says so — and reviewed status was conferred only by a named human reviewer, Dr Umar Ahmad, whose approval is recorded against the specific revision reviewed. Drafting provenance and review approval are kept as separate fields precisely so that neither can be mistaken for the other.

Corrections and withdrawal

Readers can report an error from any entry. A managing editor may withdraw inaccurate material promptly, recording the reason; withdrawn entries remain at their URLs with an explanation and, where appropriate, a link to a replacement. Renamed entries redirect permanently from their previous address.
