Accessibility

The target is WCAG 2.2 level AA. This statement describes what has actually been checked; it is not a certification.

Built-in provisions

  • Semantic headings and landmarks, a skip link, and keyboard-operable navigation, search and forms.
  • Visible keyboard focus, labelled form fields, and result counts announced through a live region.
  • Readable layouts at 360, 768 and 1440 pixels and at 200 per cent text zoom.
  • Definitions remain available when images, audio or JavaScript do not load: the dictionary page lists every entry as an ordinary link, and each term has its own page.
  • A print stylesheet that keeps the definition, references, citation and revision details without navigation clutter.

Known limitations

Search filtering, autocomplete and pagination require JavaScript; without it the full A to Z list is still browsable. Automated and manual testing against the full WCAG 2.2 AA checklist, including screen-reader testing on more than one platform, is scheduled before public launch and the findings will be recorded here.

Feedback

If something on this site is difficult to use with assistive technology, please tell us through Contact and describe the page and the technology you were using.
