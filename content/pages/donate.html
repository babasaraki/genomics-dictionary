What donations do not buy

Financial support gives no influence over scientific content, review outcomes, the choice of terms or the wording of any entry. No donor is named in an entry. Donors may decline public acknowledgement entirely, and any newsletter consent is separate from a contribution and off by default.

Transparency

Once fundraising is active, the project intends to publish a periodic summary of funds received and how they were spent, without exposing donor information. No donor counts, totals or funding targets are displayed until the underlying records are accurate and their disclosure has been authorised.

See also the donation information policy.
