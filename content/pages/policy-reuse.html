Terms and reuse

The dictionary is intended to be reusable for teaching and study. The exact licence is a decision for the project owner and is not yet in force.

Licence status

No reuse licence has been approved yet. Until the owner approves one, content on this site is published for reading and study; ask before redistributing it. The proposed starting position is a Creative Commons attribution licence for original project-written definitions, with third-party material excluded.

Third-party material

Illustrations, quotations and data obtained from other sources keep their own rights and licences and do not inherit any site-wide licence. Each such item carries its own source, creator and permission record.

Citing an entry

Every entry page shows a suggested citation including the term, the dictionary name, the revision number, the date and the entry URL. The revision number matters: it identifies the exact text that was reviewed and approved, which may differ from a later revision of the same entry.

Accuracy and liability

Entries explain concepts for education. They are not clinical, diagnostic, legal or regulatory advice and must not be used to interpret an individual's results. Report suspected errors through Report an error.
