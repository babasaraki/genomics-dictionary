Privacy

The dictionary is readable without an account. This page states plainly what the current static site does and does not do.

What this site collects

The published site is a set of static files. It sets no cookies, runs no advertising, and includes no analytics or third-party tracking script. Standard server request logs may be kept by the hosting provider; their retention is governed by that provider.

Forms

The suggestion, correction and contact forms submit to a form service only once the project owner has configured one. Until then the forms display that no submission service is connected rather than pretending to store a message. Forms collect only the fields needed to act on a request.

Please do not submit identifiable patient information, genetic test reports, clinical records or raw genomic data through any form on this site.

Donations

No payment details are collected by this website. If a hosted donation provider is configured, payment is completed on the provider's own secure page under the provider's privacy terms.
